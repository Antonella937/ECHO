# Blackthorne Academy — Character story bible (editable drafts)

These are **private character dossiers**, not universal world knowledge. Every character is independently playable as a protagonist. No character automatically knows another character's private conflict or secret. These are proposed narrative hooks, not compulsory outcomes.

## Sebastian Valemont — Valtheris
**Public persona:** charismatic, brave, exceptional duelist, seemingly arrogant.
**Private contradiction:** fearless against danger, terrified of disappointing loved ones or losing admiration. His prestigious family treats excellence as a minimum. He can protect others but struggles to accept care.
**Personal arc:** learn that defeat and vulnerability do not make him unworthy.
**Independent plot:** pressure from his family, a consequential duel, and the choice between reputation and integrity.
**Relationships:** protective behavior may clash with Alanis's need for autonomy. He can disagree with Ophelia about whether an unverified warning justifies action.
**Knowledge:** only Sebastian initially knows the full depth of his insecurity.

## Lucien Moreau — Noctis
**Public persona:** analytical, calm, private, knowledgeable about perception and resonance.
**Private contradiction:** expertly detects magical distortions but struggles to name his own feelings; uses understanding as a substitute for control.
**Personal arc:** accept uncertainty and emotional vulnerability.
**Independent plot:** suspicious inconsistencies in archival memories; possible alteration of one of his own memories is a *story hook*, not a settled fact.
**Relationships:** may become Nela Ashbourne's patient peer mentor on perception; the connection can slowly develop into trust or romance, but is not mandatory. Ezra sees through his emotional evasions.
**Knowledge:** knows only fragments about Aster and a possible lost tradition, not Ophelia's secret birth name.

## Theodore Hawthorne — Verdanth
**Public persona:** steady, considerate, responsible alchemist.
**Private contradiction:** tells everyone to respect limits while pushing his own research beyond safe boundaries.
**Personal arc:** learn that not every loss can or should be reversed.
**Independent plot:** a possible earlier alchemical mistake involving someone close to him; investigation of forbidden restoration techniques. Whether the mistake happened is deliberately open for the chosen player.
**Relationships:** hiding risky research may conflict with Vivienne's insistence on autonomy.
**Knowledge:** partial alchemical clues to Aster and an unknown tradition, not their definitive meaning.

## Ezra — Vesperis
**Public persona:** funny, rebellious, spontaneous, mischievous.
**Private contradiction:** detects emotional shifts through resonant magic but jokes to avoid discussing his own feelings; fears being abandoned when he is no longer entertaining.
**Personal arc:** allow himself to be known beyond his humor.
**Independent plot:** his family undervalues resonant magic; he can challenge their judgment without needing another protagonist.
**Relationships:** initially close and chaotic friends with Nela and Vivienne; can tease Lucien's attempts to rationalize emotions.
**Knowledge:** no automatic access to others' private visions or investigations.

## Vivienne Blackwell — Verdanth
**Public persona:** bold, independent, rule-breaking, confident.
**Private contradiction:** fights for freedom but may become controlling when afraid of losing someone.
**Personal arc:** distinguish caring for someone from making decisions for them.
**Independent plot:** a family-defined future that she wants to reject.
**Relationships:** close chaotic friendship with Nela and Ezra; conflicts with Theodore if he withholds risky choices from her.
**Knowledge:** no automatic access to Theodore's private research.

## Alanis Fairchild — Verdanth
**Public persona:** gentle, reserved, empathic, considerate.
**Private contradiction:** deeply frustrated by being underestimated; protects others fiercely but struggles to advocate for herself.
**Personal arc:** set boundaries and express anger without equating either with cruelty.
**Independent plot:** unusually intense bond magic can respond to betrayal or fear, creating decisions about consent and emotional responsibility.
**Relationships:** friends with Nela and Vivienne, but initially less involved in their chaos; may push back against Sebastian's overprotection.
**Knowledge:** no automatic access to the other characters' secrets.

## Ophelia Ashbourne — publicly Nela Ashbourne — Vesperis
**Public persona:** expressive, daring, creative, selectively sociable.
**Private contradiction:** wants to protect others through visions of possible futures but cannot know which futures will occur, or whether intervening will help.
**Personal arc:** learn to live with uncertainty without carrying responsibility for every possibility.
**Independent plot:** discover why the birth name **Ophelia** was hidden, the nature of her manifestations and visions, and possible connections to Aster. Her **surname Ashbourne is public**; only her first name is secret.
**Relationships:** friendship with Ezra and Vivienne; optional slow-burn peer mentorship with Lucien about perception and memory.
**Knowledge:** does not know the full origin of her power or the truth about Aster.

## Shared world narrative rules
1. All seven characters are viable, independently playable leads. No one is required to revolve around Ophelia.
2. Distinguish **public reputation**, **private contradiction**, **unverified past**, and **player-chosen development**.
3. Do not turn optional backstory hooks into confirmed canon without player approval.
4. Secrets are private to their holders until earned through in-story disclosure, investigation or approved journal permissions.
5. Character ownership and AI autonomy must be respected: a human-controlled protagonist is not autonomously played by the AI.
6. Relationships can evolve differently in each imported or shared universe; avoid mandatory romances and fixed outcomes.
7. Magical knowledge is not mind-reading. Characters may suspect, investigate and be wrong.
