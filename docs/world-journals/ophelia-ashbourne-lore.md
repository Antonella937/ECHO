# Ophelia Ashbourne — Private character lore (draft)

## Identity
Ophelia Ashbourne publicly introduces herself as **Nela Ashbourne**. Her surname is NOT secret; her birth name **Ophelia** is. She intentionally uses Nela so other students and faculty do not learn her true first name. Her family may have concealed information about why the name matters, potentially involving ancient name-based resonance and the Fracture of Aster; this is an unresolved hypothesis, not established canon. Avoid revealing the name Ophelia to characters without an in-story discovery.

## Magic
Her primary affinity is artistic manifestation: drawings can move, paintings can change, and objects can retain impressions of past events. Her visions are not fixed prophecy: they show fragmented *possible* futures of differing likelihoods. A frightening vision may never occur. Attempts to force clarity can superimpose incompatible possibilities, making interpretation less reliable.

## Costs and moral conflict
Spontaneous visions may cause deja vu, dizziness and pressure behind the eyes. Forced visions can bring migraine, nausea, trembling, exhaustion, sensory confusion and apparent memories of conversations that never happened. She struggles with whether to intervene or remain silent: intervention might prevent harm, cause harm, or respond to an outcome that would never have happened. She conceals her visions partly to protect her privacy and partly because she fears others acting on unreliable information.

## Lucien relationship arc
Lucien is knowledgeable about perception, resonance and memory phenomena, but he is not omniscient. Ophelia approaches him with ostensibly academic questions about distinguishing real memories from altered perception. He offers techniques for observation, grounding, recording evidence and evaluating magical traces, without demanding disclosure. Their early dynamic is a peer mentorship that develops gradually into mutual investigation, trust and possible slow-burn romance. Lucien initially knows her as Nela Ashbourne; do not assume he knows her birth name Ophelia or the full nature of her visions.

## Narrative safeguards
This is private planning lore, not common world knowledge. The possible connection between her true first name and the Fracture is an unresolved mystery, not established proof of guilt or a definitive explanation of her power. Lucien and Theodore hold separate partial clues; Elowen knows more but does not disclose everything. Do not make Ophelia invincible or treat every vision as a true prophecy.
