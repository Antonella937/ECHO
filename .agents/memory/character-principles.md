---
name: ECHO character principles
description: Product rules for progressively designed people, relationships, routines and memory without premature autonomy.
---

ECHO characters are people within a social universe, not just chatbots. Quick creation must work with very little information, followed by optional, progressively editable identity, personality/voice, relationships, routine, social behavior, memory and current state. Manual editing must always remain possible, including after any future AI proposal.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants to start quickly and refine a character little by little, with coherent personality, memory and social relationships.

**How to apply:** Never require an exhaustive ficha, completed progress indicator or connected AI service before an owner can create and manually operate an account.

Relationships with the owner are conceptually distinct from relationships with Nela or any other active character. Routines express likelihood/context in a character's timezone, not inflexible robotic schedules. Silence and delayed replies are legitimate behavior. Location, mood and company must not be presented as autonomous observations when only manually supplied or routine-derived.

**Why:** The user asked what relationship characters have “con vos,” daily routines and realistic time to reply while retaining the owner/active-identity separation.

**How to apply:** Future engines should consume this context without inventing activity or overriding manual corrections merely because a scheduled time arrived.

Permanent and relationship memories remain owner-editable; recent memory should be a bounded contextual index of real events, not an unbounded duplicate transcript. Pruning that index must never delete source posts, stories or DMs. Deleting a character must not orphan any history or relationships; archive instead, including if concurrent activity makes an initially safe deletion stale.

**Why:** The user wants sufficient social coherence without storing absolutely everything forever, while preserving the existing universe and cross-device data.

**How to apply:** Future summarization/promotion must distinguish facts from guesses and leave durable facts reviewable. Copies must not gain fictitious interaction history or inherited external connections.

For this stage, Kindroid, visual generation, persistent visual identity, TTS and real autonomous posts/DMs are explicitly out of scope. Without a configured text provider, assisted creation must say it is unavailable, retain an editable brief and allow manual continuation.

**Why:** The user explicitly allowed the honest dependency boundary and prohibited accidental proprietary coupling or pretending generation works.

**How to apply:** Do not silently replace AI generation with templates or natural-language parsing branded as AI, and do not connect a provider without a new request.
