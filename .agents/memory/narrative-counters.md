---
name: Narrative counters
description: Product rule separating editable fictional scale from real social activity.
---

Narrative bases for post likes/comments, character followers/following and Story views are additive presentation values. Editing a base must never create or remove actual likes, comments, follows or viewers, and clearing a base must retain real activity.

**Why:** The creator explicitly requires “base narrativa + interacciones reales”, with real Story views tracked separately and without duplication or loss.

**How to apply:** Keep the base distinct from interaction identities/history, preserve legacy content without inventing participants, and verify the total and underlying real activity independently after edits and reloads. Story views keep the existing unique-viewer-per-character meaning, not an impression counter for repeated opens.
